What is the free online review?

It is a review of your website, your Google listing and your social media. You find out what is working, what is missing, and what to fix first. It is free, and you can book it online.

How much does it cost?

Every project gets a written quote before any work begins, so you know the price up front. One-time projects are a single fee. Monthly services are billed each month.

What is the difference between a one-time project and a monthly service?

A one-time project builds something once, such as your branding, your website or your Google listing. A monthly service is ongoing work: keeping your business visible on Google, posting on social media, or managing ads.

Can you help my business show up on Google and Google Maps?

Yes. The Website and Google Listing project sets up or completes your Google Business Profile, which is what appears on Google Maps. The monthly Google Visibility service keeps your listing and website up to date so more local customers find you.

Can you help my business show up in AI search, such as ChatGPT?

Yes. AI search tools answer from pages that state clearly who a business is, what it offers and where it works. Zur Digital writes and structures that information on your website so those tools can read and quote it.

Do you run Google and Facebook/Instagram ads, and who pays for the ad budget?

Yes. Zur Digital sets up and manages ads on Google, Facebook and Instagram. You pay the ad budget directly to Google or Meta, and the management fee is separate.
