Privacy Policy

A short, plain-language explanation of what this site collects and how I use it.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

About this website

This site is a static web page hosted by Netlify. I use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to see how people use the site—for example, which pages they visit and whether they send a message. These tools may use cookies. I do not use this information to advertise to you, and I do not sell it.

When you send a message

The contact form is provided by Netlify Forms. If you use it, it collects the name, email address, and message you type, and I use that information only to reply to you. I do not sell your information, and I do not share it except as needed to operate the form or if the law requires it.

If you want me to delete a message you sent, email me at krisztina@zurdigital.com or use the contact form and ask me to delete it.

When you book a meeting

Meeting times are booked through Calendly. If you book a time, Calendly collects the information you give them, and Calendly's privacy policy applies.

Technical logs

Netlify, which hosts this site, may keep standard server logs (such as your IP address and the pages requested) to operate and secure the site. I do not use those logs to identify you or to advertise to you.

Questions

If you have a question about this policy, email me at krisztina@zurdigital.com or use the contact form on the home page.
